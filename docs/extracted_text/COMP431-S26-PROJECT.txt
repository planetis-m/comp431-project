Semester Project

General Description
Students will work in teams of 3–4 members to design, develop, and evaluate
a practical computer security solution that applies the concepts and techniques studied in this course. The project must involve the implementation
or simulation of a security mechanism, tool, or system, rather than a purely
theoretical or literature-based study. Projects should address a realistic security problem and demonstrate the use of appropriate techniques such as
cryptography, authentication and access control, malware analysis, secure
coding, intrusion detection, firewalls, or layered defense strategies. Each
team is expected to produce a working prototype, conduct testing or attack
simulations, and evaluate the effectiveness of their solution. Emphasis will
be placed on correct design, security awareness, technical depth, and clear
presentation of results.

Project Requirements
Each team must:
• Work in groups of 3-4 students
• Design and implement a functional prototype or simulator
